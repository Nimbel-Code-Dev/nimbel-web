# FAMBA — Travel health platform with Hospital Clínic de Barcelona

**From losing sight of travelers the moment they leave the clinic to supporting 8,000 travelers throughout their trip.**

8,000 users · 5 active centers · 4 EU countries (Spain, Italy, Belgium and Germany)

## The problem
After COVID-19, the International Health Service at Hospital Clínic de Barcelona asked itself a question: could the first symptoms arriving from abroad have been detected earlier? Travel clinics see people before they leave: they vaccinate them and give them advice. But once the traveler is gone, the clinic is blind: it doesn't know whether they fall ill, come back well or what happened to them. And travelers are on their own if something goes wrong: they don't know which doctor to see, whether the country's health system can be trusted or how much it will cost.

## When we arrived
The project had spent almost two years trying to get a working version out with another provider. Hospital Clínic needed it ready by a date that could not move. With our team, we worked out which path would get us there on time and rethought the architecture from the ground up, built to grow. Within 3 months of joining, FAMBA had its first version.

## What changed
With FAMBA, travelers chat with the same medical team that vaccinated them, who already know their profile, 365 days a year. They can send photos and log symptoms with the help of AI, and they get alerts if there is an outbreak in the area. Hospital Clínic follows each case until the traveler recovers and gains a tool for epidemiological surveillance.

## How we work
The first version was the starting point. Since then, the platform has evolved as the service's team identifies new needs. Because we know the healthcare sector, we don't stop at technology: we bring product, usability and business judgment. And we designed the architecture around where the service is heading, not only what is asked for today. That way, each new step doesn't force us to redo the previous one.

## Funding
FAMBA is backed by CaixaImpulse, the health innovation program of the "la Caixa" Foundation, which awarded it its second phase.

## What we did
Design and development of the mobile app and the backend, and technical leadership of the product. Patient onboarding via QR code for a study with European recruitment centers, trip management with medical validation, and integrated payments.

## Data protection
- Health data treated as a special category (GDPR Art. 9)
- Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) carried out
- Data processing agreement (DPA) signed
- Data hosted in the EU (Belgium)
- Serverless architecture with a versioned API to deploy without disrupting patients
